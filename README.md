The Internet in One Word — one word offered as a description of the whole internet, funny and fitting at once, judged two at a time by a comedy editor.

## The entry

> doomscrolling

13 characters, one word, plain ASCII — inside the 1–30 character gate.

## Why this word

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — a single word describing the whole internet: no phrase, no sentence, no list.
2. **Earns a laugh by itself** — the joke is the word itself: the endless thumb-flick through bad news at 2 a.m. that everybody recognises the moment they read it.
3. **True about the whole internet** — not one app, one site or one mood: the feed never ends, the news never cheers up, and the reader never meant to stay this long.
4. **Surprises** — it names the reader's own behaviour instead of the internet's content. "Cat videos" or "arguments" would describe what is on it; this word describes what it does to you.
5. **Lands in one beat** — spelled correctly, instantly readable, short enough to hit before the reader thinks.
